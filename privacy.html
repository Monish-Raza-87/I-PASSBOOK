Privacy Notice

I-PASSBOOK — Indrones Product After Sales Service Book

Last updated 9 October 2026

This notice explains what I-PASSBOOK holds about you, where it is kept and who can see it. It follows Indrones Solutions Pvt. Ltd.'s published privacy policy and describes only what this application actually does.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Indrones Solutions Private Limited, L-3039, Akshar Business Park, Sector 25, Vashi, Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra 400703, India. For anything in this notice — questions, corrections, or a request to see or remove your data — write to info@indrones.com or call +91 9594160160.

2. What I-PASSBOOK holds

  • About you: your name, work email address, telephone number, the company you work for, and your role.
  • About your aircraft and your requests: the tickets raised against your machine — the fault reported, the work carried out, inspection and service records, and the dates on them.
  • Files you upload: photographs, documents and reports attached to a ticket as evidence.
  • Sign-in records: the date and time you signed in, the account used, and the device label from your browser. These exist so the desk can tell who did what, and to keep your account safe.

The application holds nothing about your private life, and nothing is bought in from data brokers. It holds what the after-sales relationship needs and no more.

3. Where it is kept

Your data is stored in Google Drive and Google Sheets owned by Indrones, reached through a private Google Apps Script service. Access to that service is token-controlled: every request must carry a signed-in session.

I-PASSBOOK carries no third-party analytics, no advertising, and no tracking pixels. Nothing about your use of it is sold, traded or passed to anyone outside Indrones, except where the law requires it.

4. Who can see it

  • Indrones staff signed in to the application can read the tickets. This is deliberate: the whole after-sales team works from one record.
  • A customer account is scoped to one company and can read that company's tickets only — never another customer's.
  • You can read the tickets belonging to the company your account is linked to.

Editing is more tightly controlled than reading: it is granted by department, not to everybody. The application decides what you may see and change from your own signed-in account, never from anything you can type into a link.

5. How long it is kept

Ticket records are kept for the life of the aircraft relationship and are archived, not deleted, when a ticket is closed — they may be needed for warranty or regulatory reasons. The audit trail of changes is kept for at least 400 days, and longer where the ticket is still open.

Nightly backups are taken and rotated on a daily, weekly, monthly and yearly cycle. Old backups are removed automatically; your current data is not.

6. Cookies and storage on your device

I-PASSBOOK sets no tracking cookies. Your browser stores three small things so the app works: your sign-in session, your light/dark choice, and your language choice. The session lasts one working day and is never extended by use. Signing out clears it.

If you enable quick unlock with a fingerprint or a pattern, the unlock is checked on your own device, and only after it succeeds does the app contact the server.

7. Your choices

  • Ask to see what is held about you.
  • Ask for anything inaccurate to be corrected.
  • Ask for your account to be closed. Records that Indrones must keep for warranty or legal reasons will be retained for that purpose, and we will tell you which ones and why.

Write to info@indrones.com, or speak to your Customer Relations contact.

8. Changes to this notice

If this notice changes in a way that affects you, the app will show it. The date at the top of this page is the version you are reading.
