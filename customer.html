    How access works

    This space is for customers who have bought from us. There is no public sign-up — an account is created for you when your aircraft is handed over, so that the record you see is only ever your own.

    1. We onboard youWhen your order is delivered, we set you up in the portal and send an invitation to your official email address.
    2. You set your credentialsOn your own space, press “First time here?” and we email you a code. Enter it with the password you want — nobody at Indrones chooses it for you, and nobody here can read it.
    3. You use it from then onEvery request, every report, every dispatch note for your fleet — under one sign-in, for the life of the aircraft.

    What happens when you raise a request

    Every request gets an IR number the moment it reaches us, and it is worked through the same ten stages every time. You can watch it move.

    1. OpenYour report has reached the service desk and is waiting to be taken up.
    2. InwardThe aircraft or component is on its way to us, and we log it in when it arrives.
    3. InspectionWe look over the unit and record its condition as received.
    4. InvestigationOur engineers find the cause and write up what they found.
    5. ProductionThe repair or replacement is carried out.
    6. Quality TestThe work is tested before it leaves us.
    7. PDI / DispatchFinal checks, packing, and the dispatch note to you.
    8. DeliveredIt is back with you. The request is closed.

    Two of the ten are not steps in that sequence: On Hold means work is paused — usually waiting on a part or on your approval — and Remote Support means we are helping you without the unit coming in.
